Why FastBoi?

FastBoi was created in 2020 by a group of amateur runners who made the journey from zero to marathon themselves. We have helped thousands of people start running and stick with it. Our plans are written in plain language — no sports jargon, no excessive theory. We collaborate with physiotherapists and sports dietitians so every piece of advice is sound and safe.
